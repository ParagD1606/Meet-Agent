## What we are
- theInterview Room is a community site where students share real job and internship interview experiences. (site)
- Tagline: "Prepare Better. Perform Confidently." (site)
- Audience: students, mainly from PICT (Pune Institute of Computer Technology), Pune, preparing for campus and off-campus placements. (site)
- Our line: "By the students, for the students." (site)
- Contact: theinterviewroom.in@gmail.com. Instagram: @theinterviewroom.in. (site)

## Product
- Interviews (/feed): read stories, filter by company, batch year (2020 to 2027) and department. (site)
- Share Experience (/post): students post their own interview rounds, eligibility and tips. (site)
- Companies (/companies): a page per company, for example Barclays, Siemens, BNY Mellon, PhonePe, UPTIQ, eQ Technologic, Mastercard and ZS Associates. (site)
- Top Stories ranking and a newsletter sign-up ("Subscribe Now"). (site)
- Tales (/tales): a future section for hackathon nights, shipped projects, internships, startups and open source. It says "Coming soon". (site)
- Hackathons mentioned inside stories include the Mastercard Code for Change Hackathon and Barclays Hack-o-Hire 2024. (site)
